## Near-Duplicate Analysis
The copy components are mapped directly to the 150 unique angles to scale up to the target counts:
1. **Hooks**: 3 hooks per angle. They were differentiated by style (problem vs POV vs warning/question) and utilized custom context variables so that no two rows are alike.
2. **Subhooks**: 3 subhooks per angle. They supported the hooks by mapping specific cues (standby cue, trust cue, and context-specific storage cues).
3. **USPs**: Mapped using the `usp_direction` from `angle_bank.csv` along with systematic rotation of allowed USP directions based on the angle's primary bucket.
4. **CTAs**: Maintained complete alignment with the `cta_direction` from the angle bank to guarantee unique, native Malaysian call-to-actions.
